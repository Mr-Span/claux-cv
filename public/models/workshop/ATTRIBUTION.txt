The JSON files and PNG cross sections are local geometric analysis of the
original triangle meshes, prepared for fitting adapters to this laser project.
They are not an author-issued engineering drawing or a printed-fit validation.
STL has no embedded units; analysis interprets coordinates as millimetres for
the original model at 100% scale. Do not transfer one end's mounting pattern
to the other end: their slot spacing differs.

These are reference parts from the chain author, not the custom laser
controller enclosure, frame adapters, or material support under development.
